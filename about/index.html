About

The calm side of
your busy week.

I’m Kelsey John, a virtual PA and lifestyle concierge based in Essex. After more than a decade supporting senior leaders in luxury fashion, I now help busy professionals, business owners and families get the admin off their plate—and their evenings back.

Essex, UK :)

From one senior leader’s
right hand to yours

For over eleven years—starting in 2015—I worked behind the scenes supporting senior leaders across the luxury fashion industry: back-to-back diaries, international travel, last-minute venue requests and fast-moving inboxes. I learned early on that the best assistant is the one you never have to think twice about.

Becoming a mum to Freddie and Sadie made me see time differently. I started Virtually Kelsey to bring that same calm, discreet executive support—alongside a trusted black book of lifestyle connections—to busy founders, professionals and families across the UK.

Today I deliberately work with a handful of clients at a time. It means I get to know your business, your household and your tone of voice properly, and I never have to ask twice. When I’m not clearing inboxes or coordinating trips, I’m out in Essex with Freddie, Sadie and a fresh coffee in hand.
