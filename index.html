How do I get started with your services?

To get started, simply contact me and we'll schedule an initial consultation to discuss your needs and determine the best plan for you. The consultation is free of charge.

How do you ensure the confidentiality of my information?

I take confidentiality very seriously. All information shared with me is kept strictly confidential and is only used for the purposes of providing the agreed-upon services

What are your working hours and availability?

I strive to be flexible and accommodating to my clients' needs. My standard working hours are 9 AM - 7 PM CET, but I am available for urgent tasks outside these hours by prior arrangement. For 24/7 support, please consider my Custom Plan.

How do you handle communication and task management?

I use various tools for communication and task management, such as email, instant messaging apps, and project management platforms like Trello or Asana. We'll discuss and agree on the best method of communication and tools that suit your workflow during our initial consultation.

What if I need to cancel or change my plan?

I understand that circumstances can change. If you need to cancel or change your plan, please contact me as soon as possible. For cancellations, I require a 30-day notice". You can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time to better suit your needs.
